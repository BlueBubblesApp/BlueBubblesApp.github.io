---
question: "How can I make sure my Mac starts up after a power failure?"
category: other
order: 3
---
<p>In System Preferences, you must turn on the "Automatically turn back on after a power failure" option in order for your Mac to reboot after a power failure.
    In addition, turn on the "Startup with macOS" option in the BlueBubbles settings to make sure it runs when you login.</p>
