<p>Yes! You will need to set up a macOS VM (Virtual Machine) on either Windows or Linux. We have a handful of guides in
    our <a href="https://docs.bluebubbles.app/server/advanced/running-a-macos-vm" rel="noopener">documentation</a>,
    as well as on our <a href="https://www.reddit.com/r/BlueBubbles/" rel="noopener">subreddit</a>. We can also assist with any issues such as iMessage activation, in our Discord.</p>
<p>Disclaimer: This is meant to be done on Apple Hardware</p>
